# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standard

EveryPivot&trade; should feel welcoming to analysts, engineers, researchers, and contributors from different backgrounds and experience levels.

We ask contributors to:
- be respectful and constructive
- assume good intent while still giving honest technical feedback
- explain disagreements with evidence and clarity
- avoid harassment, intimidation, or dismissive behavior
- keep discussion focused on improving the corpus and the community

## Expected Behavior

- thoughtful technical criticism
- curiosity and collaboration
- clear attribution of uncertainty and limitations
- patience with newcomers
- respect for maintainers, contributors, and downstream users

## Unacceptable Behavior

- personal attacks
- harassment or intimidation
- hostile or demeaning language
- repeated bad-faith argument
- publishing private information without consent

## Maintainer Role

Maintainers are responsible for preserving a productive, respectful project environment.

They may moderate discussion, ask for changes in tone or behavior, or remove contributions that undermine the health of the project.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to `github@everypivot.io`.

Please include enough context for maintainers to understand the issue, but do
not include private information about other people unless it is necessary to
explain the concern. Maintainers will review reports in good faith and take
proportionate action to protect the project community.
