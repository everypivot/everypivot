# License FAQ

This FAQ explains EveryPivot's licensing model in plain English. It is not
legal advice; the controlling terms are in `LICENSE-CODE` and `LICENSE-DATA`.

## What license applies?

EveryPivot uses a split license model:

- Code, schemas, tooling, docs, build scripts, adapters, generated adapter
  queries, and site assets are licensed under Apache-2.0.
- The pattern corpus in `graph-pivots/` and fixtures in `fixtures/` are
  licensed under CC BY 4.0.

## Can I use EveryPivot commercially?

Yes. Commercial use is allowed.

## Can I use EveryPivot in a SaaS or product?

Yes. You may use EveryPivot in commercial products, hosted services, internal
platforms, consulting deliverables, and downstream analysis tools, provided you
follow the license terms that apply to the material you use.

## Can my private overlays remain private?

Yes. Internal overlays, private mappings, customer-specific scoring, local
casework, and proprietary product integrations can remain private.

## Can downstream additions be proprietary?

Yes. CC BY 4.0 does not require your additions, private overlays, product
logic, or modified corpus material to be open licensed or contributed back to
EveryPivot.

You must still preserve attribution for original EveryPivot material, mark
changes where required, avoid implying endorsement, and respect the EveryPivot
trademark.

## Can downstream terms remove EveryPivot's CC BY rights?

No. A downstream product can apply its own terms to its own additions and
implementation, but it cannot remove the CC BY 4.0 rights attached to original
EveryPivot corpus material.

## Do I have to contribute improvements back?

No. Contributions back to EveryPivot are encouraged, but not required by the
license.

## How should I attribute EveryPivot?

Use this attribution string where you list third-party notices, data sources,
or open-source components:

```text
Pattern definitions and tooling from EveryPivot Project,
used under Apache-2.0 (code) and CC BY 4.0 (patterns).
```

If you publish modified corpus material, also indicate that you made changes.

## Can I call my fork or product EveryPivot?

No. The licenses cover code and corpus material; they do not grant trademark
rights. You may accurately state that your work uses or is based on EveryPivot,
but do not name your fork, hosted service, or competing product EveryPivot.

## Is alternative licensing available?

No alternative licensing or commercial exception process exists at this stage.
