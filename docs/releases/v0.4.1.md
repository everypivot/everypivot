# EveryPivot v0.4.1 Release Notes

Release date: 2026-06-01

v0.4.1 is a bounded CTI pilot-promotion release. It moves the supply-chain
SBOM dependency and cloud/SaaS tenant-to-auth-event pilots from
`graph-pivots/deferred/` to `graph-pivots/validated/`, lands two synthetic
traversal evidence packs under `fixtures/examples/`, and records a durable
public review trail aligned with `docs/CTI_PROMOTION_BOUNDARIES.md`. The
`pivot-pattern` schema version is unchanged at v1.4; the validated lane grows
by two promoted patterns.

## Added

- `graph-pivots/validated/SUPPLY_SBOM_DEPENDENCY_TO_PRODUCTS.yaml` promoted
  from `deferred/`, expanded to the v1.4 schema surface (review block,
  `robustness_class`, `assessment.subject_role` / `assessment.object_role`),
  and characterised as `precision_tier: low` with SBOM-noise hazards
  enumerated in full (transitive/dev-only, vendored, version-range drift,
  ubiquitous base libraries, stale historical edges).
- `fixtures/examples/supply_sbom_dependency_to_products.evidence.json`
  synthetic traversal evidence pack covering six roles: `positive`,
  `weak_positive`, `cautionary_negative`, two `suppression` traversals for
  the stale and ubiquitous-base-library cases, and `high_cardinality`. All
  identifiers, sources, and timestamps are synthetic.
- `graph-pivots/validated/CTI_CLOUD_TENANT_UID_TO_AUTH_EVENTS.yaml` promoted
  from `deferred/`, trimmed to a single-hop pilot scope (`contains_auth_event`
  only; the `associated_session` second hop is excluded pending a separate
  multi-hop review). Target reduced from `auth:event|auth:session` to
  `auth:event`. `robustness_class` reduced from `multi_hop_inference` to
  `enumeration` consistent with the single-hop scope. `precision_tier` set to
  `low` and `basis` set to `assessed` for the validated bar. v1.4 review block
  added.
- `fixtures/examples/cloud_tenant_uid_to_auth_events.evidence.json`
  synthetic traversal evidence pack covering six roles: `positive`,
  `weak_positive`, `cautionary_negative`, two `suppression` traversals for
  the stale historical and provider-or-healthcheck cases, and
  `high_cardinality` with four-event fan-out. All identifiers, sources, and
  timestamps are synthetic.
- `docs/CTI_PROMOTION_BOUNDARIES.md` documents the public boundary between CTI
  graph-pivot mechanics and assessment, attribution, confidence, operational
  effect, or private review state.
- `tools/check_cti_promotion_lint.rb` and
  `tools/test_cti_promotion_lint.rb` add a promotion-blocking CTI safety gate
  for public CTI pattern and fixture material.

## Changed

- CTI pilot promotion gates closed for the two promoted patterns. The relevant
  blocking checks pass on the new layout: `tools/check_cti_promotion_lint.rb`,
  `tools/validate_pivots.rb`, and `tools/check_fixture_suite.rb` (four
  traversal evidence examples now live under `fixtures/examples/`).
- `docs/RELATION_CATALOG.md` was refreshed for the single-hop cloud/SaaS
  promotion scope. The removed `associated_session` second hop and
  `auth:event|auth:session` target form are no longer counted in the current
  public inventory.

## Non-Assertion Statement

This release does not assert attribution, maliciousness, compromise, runtime
confidence, operational effect, ownership, impact, or final assessment for any
real package, project, maintainer, tenant, user, session, organisation, or
incident. The validated lifecycle state of
`SUPPLY_SBOM_DEPENDENCY_TO_PRODUCTS` and
`CTI_CLOUD_TENANT_UID_TO_AUTH_EVENTS` reflects only that each pattern's
precision claim is honest, its hazards are concrete, its negative-node
controls target the obvious failure modes, and synthetic evidence demonstrates
the noise handling. Dependency presence remains candidate context, not runtime
reachability; tenant-event context remains a scoped observation, not identity
validation, ownership, or current access.
