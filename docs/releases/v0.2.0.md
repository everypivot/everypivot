# EveryPivot v0.2.0 Release Notes

Release date: 2026-05-24

v0.2.0 is the first evidence and usability release. It adds traversal evidence
examples and relation-catalog guidance without expanding the pattern corpus or
changing the `pivot-pattern` schema version.

## Added

- Traversal evidence-pack roles for positive, weak-positive,
  cautionary-positive, cautionary-negative, negative, suppression, and
  high-cardinality examples.
- Two synthetic traversal evidence packs:
  - `OSINT_SSH_HOSTKEY_CLUSTER`, covering exact host-key reuse, stale-edge
    suppression, shared-hosting suppression, and a different-key negative
    control.
  - `CTI_SAMPLE_IMPHASH_CLUSTER`, covering import-hash clustering,
    weak-positive corroboration, common-packer suppression, and
    high-cardinality handling.
- Fixture-suite checks for traversal evidence examples, including referenced
  validated pattern IDs, role coverage, blocked assertions, and basic graph
  consistency.
- `docs/RELATION_CATALOG.md`, a docs-only inventory of current source, target,
  hop relation, and hop form vocabulary with naming guidance, semantic-family
  groupings, facet vocabulary, inverse/companion candidates, deprecation notes,
  and portability-class guidance.

## Changed

- Updated promotion and contribution docs to require explicit fixture roles and
  blocked assertions when traversal evidence supports a pattern.
- Updated the first-use SSH host-key walkthrough to reference the evidence-pack
  format.

## Notes

- No corpus expansion: 176 patterns remain published.
- No schema-version change: `pivot-pattern` remains v1.4.
- No relation warning lints, sidecar metadata, schema-facing relation fields,
  or committed portability-class records are added in this release.
- `validated` continues to mean curated enough to feature publicly. It does not
  mean a runtime match is true, malicious, attributed, high confidence, or
  finally assessed.
